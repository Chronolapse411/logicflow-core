AI Architecture

Three-tier sovereign AI.

AI that runs where it makes sense — on your device first, your community second, the cloud only when you ask.

Pulse

Local — Always On

A fine-tuned Gemma 3 model that runs entirely on your CPU/GPU. Interprets scan results, answers questions, and suggests actions without any network connection.

Lives on your device. Never calls home.

Hive

Community — Opt-In

A P2P mesh where Pulse models share anonymized system-health patterns to improve collectively via federated learning. No data leaves as identifiable information.

Lives on the community mesh. Opt-in only.

Oracle

Cloud — On Demand

Gemini Live for real-time voice interaction and complex reasoning. Routed through our sovereign proxy — the API key is never in your client binary. Pro/Enterprise only.

Lives on our sovereign proxy. API key protected.
